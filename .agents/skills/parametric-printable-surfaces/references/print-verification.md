# NDN print inspection (prototype)

1. Open default STL in PrusaSlicer; confirm **160 × 160 × 24 mm** and rear face against the build plate. Confirm brim/skirt stays within the actual bed.
2. Check layer preview around all four keyhole undercuts; the relief is printable in Z, but the rear mounting lips create local overhangs and bridges.
3. Print a pocket coupon with chosen PETG filament and the intended screw, because actual tolerances vary by machine, filament, print temperature, and fastener.
4. After print: trial-insert screw head through round pocket; slide into narrow slot; check retention in all four rotations; test the intended hanging load and avoid mounting above people before validation.
5. If acoustic benefit is required, design and test a porous backing/felt layer with installation air gap. The PETG relief alone is primarily decorative / diffusively textured.
6. Record the slicing profile, material lot, print temperature, print orientation, fastener measurements, and inspection results.
